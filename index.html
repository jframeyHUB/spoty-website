Fair voting

Popularity you earn, not buy

Every vote is a real person backing you. Nobody can buy their way to the top.

  • One vote per person, per salesperson, per year. That includes paid votes.
  • Every profile is phone-verified, and only verified winners can collect a trophy.
  • Fake accounts and duplicate voters are removed, along with their votes.
  • Every winner is checked before they're announced.

Got a vote link?

Back your salesperson in one tap

Open their link, tap Apple Pay or Google Pay, done. A vote is 99p in the UK, 99¢ in the USA and Canada, and the same value in your local currency everywhere else. You don't need the app, and you can add a message of up to 300 characters.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is SPOTY free for salespeople?

Yes. Creating a profile is free, and every member gets one free vote to give another member each year.

Who can take part?

Anyone aged 18 or over who works in sales, anywhere in the world: recruitment, SaaS, car sales, property, insurance, and every other kind of selling.

How do the age awards work?

If you choose to show your age, you compete in your age band: 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54 or 55+. We only ever show your age, never your birthday.

Can members vote for each other?

Yes. Members can vote for each other, once per person per year like everyone else.

When does voting close?

Midnight UK time on 31 December 2027. Winners are revealed live in January 2028.
